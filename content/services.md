<section class="svc" id="robotics">
  <div class="svc-head">
    <h2>Robotics and autonomy</h2>
    <p>A robot is a distributed system on wheels, with a bad network connection. I help robotics teams build the infrastructure that a field deployment needs: telemetry you can trust, releases you can roll back, and data pipelines that do not break the budget.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="svc-items">
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>Fleet observability</h3>
      <p>Correlated logs, metrics, and traces from ROS 2 robots, with OpenTelemetry. Find the cause of a field failure in minutes, not days.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>CI/CD for ROS 2</h3>
      <p>Reproducible builds, automated tests, and simulation in the pipeline, so every merge is checked before it reaches a robot.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>Over-the-air updates</h3>
      <p>Staged rollouts to the fleet, with health checks and automatic rollback when a release goes wrong.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>Edge-to-cloud data</h3>
      <p>Pipelines for sensor data and bag files. Clear rules for what stays on the robot, what goes to the cloud, and for how long.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>Fleet backend and orchestration</h3>
      <p>Cloud architecture for fleet management, mission orchestration, and real-time decisions across many robots or vehicles.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="svc-item">
      <h3>Reliability and compliance</h3>
      <p>High availability, incident response, and audit trails for safety-critical and regulated operations.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
