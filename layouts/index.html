Where robotics teams get stuck

These problems come back in almost every robotics team I talk to. Each one has a known fix.

“It worked in simulation.”
A mission fails in the field and nobody can say why. I set up correlated telemetry, so you can replay what the robot did and the reason it did it.
Every release is a risk.
Updates go to robots by hand, one at a time. I build staged over-the-air rollouts with health checks and fast rollback.
Data grows faster than the fleet.
Bags and logs fill disks and buckets. I design what stays on the robot, what goes to the cloud, and what gets deleted.
The cloud bill is a surprise.
Spend grows with no clear owner. I review it line by line, remove the waste, and set guardrails so it stays down.
