How I run an engagement

You always know what is done, what is next and what I need from you.

  1. Statement of work

    Scope, deliverables, timeline and price on two pages. We both sign before work starts.

  2. Weekly update

    Every Friday, a written note: what is done, what is next, risks, and decisions I need from you.

  3. Your accounts, from day one

    All code, infrastructure and documents live in your repositories and cloud accounts. Nothing is locked to me.

  4. Handover

    A recorded walkthrough, runbooks, and two weeks of follow-up questions at no extra cost.

Questions about terms

Why do you charge for the diagnostic?

A free assessment gets a quick look. A paid one gets two weeks of focused work and a plan you can act on, with me or without me. The full fee counts toward your first sprint if you start it within 30 days.

How do payments work?

The diagnostic is paid at the start. Sprints are paid 50% at the start and 50% at handover. The monthly plan is paid in advance each month. I invoice in USD or AED.

What if the scope changes during a sprint?

We write the change down, agree a fixed price for it, and add it to the plan. There is no open-ended hourly billing.

Who owns the work?

You do. All code, configuration and documentation belong to you when you pay. I am happy to sign your NDA before we see any system.

Do you work on-site?

I work remotely with teams in all time zones. I can work on-site in the UAE, and travel for key milestones elsewhere.

How many clients do you take at a time?

A small number, so each team gets real attention. If I do not have capacity, I tell you the earliest start date on the first call.
