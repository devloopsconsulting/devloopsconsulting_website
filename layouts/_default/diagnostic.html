Questions

Why not a free assessment?

A free assessment gets a quick look and a sales pitch. A paid diagnostic gets ten days of focused work and a plan that is useful even if you never hire me. The full fee counts toward your first sprint if you start it within 30 days.

Do you need write access to our systems?

No. Read-only access is enough. I am happy to sign your NDA first.

How much of my team's time does it take?

About four hours in total: the kick-off, two or three short interviews, and the readout.

What if we are not on AWS?

I review AWS, Azure, GCP and on-premises systems, including GPU clusters.
